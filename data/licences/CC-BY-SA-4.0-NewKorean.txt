New Korean Orthography letters (data/sources/new-korean/outlines.json.gz)
=========================================================================

The letter outlines are derived from the four SVG drawings by Nanuun-saram
on Wikimedia Commons, made 2016-02-08:

  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nkchar-l.svg
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nkchar-rr.svg
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nkchar-w.svg
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nkchar-y.svg

They are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International License:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The outlines are a derivative of those drawings and are distributed under
the same license. The code points follow the proposal in

  OBAYASHI Muneaki, "Amended Proposal to add the '6 letters' (Hangul Jamo)",
  L2/19-230R, 2019-06-24,
  https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2019/19230r-six-hangul-letters.pdf

and are not assigned in Unicode 18.0.0.

The outlines are a rendering aid; they are not an encoding proposal and
carry no endorsement from the authors of the proposal or of the Commons
drawings.
